research

Cross-correlating noise recorded at two stations, day after day, turns it into a wave travelling between them. Stack enough days and the wave becomes clear, as in the trace at the top of this page.

I use these waves to measure dv/v, the tiny changes in seismic velocity that follow groundwater, stress and volcanic activity.
