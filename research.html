Research

ambient seismic noise · dv/v

so far

I'm early in this field and still exploring it.

I got interested after reading Brenguier et al. (2008, Nature Geoscience). Using only background seismic noise, they tracked tiny changes in seismic velocity under Piton de la Fournaise volcano and saw the velocity drop before its eruptions in 1999 and 2000.

Since then I've been learning about dv/v: how it is measured from noise, and what it can tell us about changes underground.

interests

  • how dv/v is measured, and how far the measurements can be trusted
  • telling real changes in the ground apart from seasonal effects like rain and temperature
  • what velocity changes can tell us about processes underground
